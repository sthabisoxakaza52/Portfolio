About Me

I'm currently studying Software Engineering at WeThinkCode in Cape Town. While most of my classmates gravitate toward front-end or full-stack work, I find myself more curious about what happens after you hit deploy.

That's where my interest in cloud engineering comes in. I love thinking about how infrastructure scales, stays secure, and supports applications in production. My focus areas include:

  • Designing reliable cloud infrastructure
  • Security best practices and IAM
  • Monitoring and observability
  • Automation with IaC tools
  • Building for scalability from the start

Right now I'm actively learning AWS, Azure, Linux, Terraform, Docker, and DevOps workflows. I build projects to reinforce concepts — not just to complete tutorials, but to really understand why things work the way they do.

Why Cloud?

Honestly, I got hooked after my first AWS course. I realized that cloud infrastructure isn't just about hosting websites — it's about architecting systems that are resilient, efficient, and secure. That's what excites me.

My long-term goal is to become a Cloud Engineer or DevOps Engineer, working on infrastructure that supports real users at scale. I'm not just chasing certifications; I want to genuinely understand the craft.
